About Anamaya

The practice of conscious living.

A name, a promise and a place — set among the oldest hills in the world, for the oldest of human intentions: to be well.

अनामय · The Name

Anāmaya: free from illness

In Sanskrit and Pali, anāmaya (अनामय) is among the most ancient words for health — not the presence of something, but the absence of disease; a body and mind at ease, unburdened. The old dictionaries gloss it simply: “free from illness, healthy, sound.”

We read that as a whole philosophy. Wellness is not accumulated — it is uncovered. It is what remains when hurry, toxins, noise and strain are gently set down. Everything at Anamaya, from the hour we wake to the food we serve, is built around that single act of setting down.

So the journey to well-being commences internally — and the sanctuary around it is only there to help.

What We Hold To

Three quiet commitments

01 · Conscious Living

Attention is the practice

Yoga, meditation, healing and naturopathy are not services here — they are trainings in attention. We teach practices you can carry into traffic, deadlines and family life, not just on a mat.

02 · The Pause

Rest is productive

We protect emptiness in the schedule the way other places protect efficiency. Silence hours, hammocks, long lunches — the nervous system repairs in the gaps, so we build the gaps.

03 · Nature as Physician

Five elements, no drugs

Following the naturopathic tradition, we work with earth, water, fire, air and ether — mud and hydro therapies, sun and breath, fasting and food — trusting the body's own healing intelligence.

The Sanctuary

A garden at the foot of the Aravallis

Anamaya sits in walled gardens on the quiet side of Amer Road, where the pink city ends and the Aravalli range begins — some of the oldest hills on earth, and some of the quietest air near Jaipur.

The grounds hold an open-air yoga shala, a meditation hall, treatment rooms for massage and nature-cure, a kitchen garden that feeds the thali, and a handful of simple rooms and kutirs for residential guests.

How It Works

Your arc at Anamaya

Whether you come for a day or for a fortnight, every stay moves through the same gentle arc.

Step 01

Arrive & listen

A welcome conversation and naturopathy consultation — history, sleep, digestion, stress, intention. We listen before we prescribe.

Step 02

Receive your rhythm

A personal daily rhythm: practices, therapies, meals and rest hours, matched to your constitution and your energy on arrival.

Step 03

Practise & restore

Yoga and meditation with the group; therapies and healing one-to-one. Days are full but never hurried.

Step 04

Carry it home

A closing consultation leaves you with a simple home practice — food notes, breath, movement — so the pause becomes a path.
