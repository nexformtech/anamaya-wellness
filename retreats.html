Practical Notes

Getting here, settling in

  • By air

    Jaipur International Airport

    About 25 km · 45–60 min by taxi. Transfers arranged on request.

  • By rail

    Jaipur Junction

    About 18 km · 40 min. The pink city is between you and the hills.

  • By road

    Amer Road, Aravalli foothills

    Past Amer fort, the city thins into hills; we are where the noise stops.

What To Bring

Travel light

  • A light shawl for evening sits

  • A journal, if you keep one
